Three simple steps, start to finish.

  1. 01

    Find a book

    Filter by grade, subject, or condition. Find the exact textbook another family in Oman has on their shelf.

  2. 02

    Reach out

    Message them on WhatsApp, call, or email directly, with no app inbox or middleman in between.

  3. 03

    Pick it up

    Arrange a quick handover in a public place. The book gets a second life and your shelf gets a little space back.

A small idea, pointed at big goals.

GreenShelf is a local act of reuse that ladders up to three of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals.

04

Quality Education

Lowering the cost of textbooks so the next student is never priced out of the books they need to learn.

UN Target 4.1

Every child completing free, equitable, quality education.

GreenShelf keeps the cost of each new school year close to zero, so no student is priced out.

12

Responsible Consumption

Reuse over re-buy, by default, keeping good books in circulation instead of in a landfill.

UN Target 12.5

Substantially reduce waste through prevention and reuse.

Every book passed on here is one not reprinted, repackaged, or sent to landfill.

13

Climate Action

Every reused book quietly avoids the carbon of printing, binding, and shipping a brand-new one.

UN Target 13.3

Build education and awareness on climate action.

Skipping one reprint avoids the carbon of paper, ink, binding, and freight.

Every June in Oman, perfectly good textbooks pile up in homes, and two streets away, a family is searching for that exact book. GreenShelf is the bridge.

Hitesh & Anshul, founders · Muscat, 2026
