A small Omani field-note

Books deserve a second life.

A calm, free, local place to pass school textbooks on at the end of the year — instead of binning them.

i.

Every June,

perfectly good textbooks

pile up in homes across Oman.

Most end up in the recycling.

Or worse.

ii.

Two streets away,

a family is searching

for the exact book

your child just outgrew.

— iii.

GreenShelf is the bridge.

No fees. No commission. No WhatsApp chaos.
Just a calm place to pass a book on.

— iv.

Three steps.
No money. No fuss.

01

Find a book.

Filter by grade, subject, condition. Find the textbook your child needs next year, listed by another family across Oman.

02

Reach out.

Message them on WhatsApp, call, or email — directly. No app inboxes, no go-between, no anonymized chat. Just two families talking.

03

Pick it up.

Arrange a quick handover in a public place. The book gets a second life, your shelf gets a little space back, and another family in Oman saves a few rials.

— v.

The books, right now.

Free for everyone. No accounts needed to look around.
