How it works

Three steps. No money. No fuss.

  1. 01

    Find a book

    Filter by grade, subject, or condition. Find the exact textbook another family in Oman has on their shelf.

  2. 02

    Reach out

    Message them on WhatsApp, call, or email — directly. No app inboxes, no anonymous chat, no middleman.

  3. 03

    Pick it up

    Arrange a quick handover in a public place. The book gets a second life and your shelf gets a little space back.

Why we built this

Every June in Oman, perfectly good textbooks pile up in homes — and two streets away, a family is searching for that exact book. GreenShelf is the bridge.

— Hitesh & Anshul, founders · Muscat, 2026
