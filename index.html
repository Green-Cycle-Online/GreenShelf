Books deserve a second life.

A calm, free, local place to pass school textbooks on at the end of the year — instead of binning them.

Every June in Oman,
perfectly good textbooks
pile up in homes.

Most go to the recycling.
Some go to the bin.
Almost none are passed on.

Two streets away,
a family is searching
for the exact same book.

— A small bet on community

GreenShelf is the bridge.

No fees. No commission. No WhatsApp chaos.
Just a calm place to pass a book on.

— Step one

Find a book.

Filter by grade, subject, condition. Find the exact textbook your child needs next year, listed by another family in Oman.

— Step two

Reach out.

Message them on WhatsApp, call, or email — directly. No app inboxes, no go-between, no anonymized chat.

— Step three

Pick it up.

Arrange a quick handover in a public place. The book gets a second life, your shelf gets a little space back.
