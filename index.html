Why GreenShelf?

At the end of every school year, perfectly good textbooks pile up in homes across Oman. Most end up in the recycling, or worse. Meanwhile, a family two streets over needs that exact book.

WhatsApp groups try to fix this, but they get messy fast. Hundreds of messages, no way to search, books lost in the scroll.

GreenShelf is the calm version. Browse, filter, find. Give freely. A small thing for your community, and one fewer book in landfill.
