Three steps, no fees, no forms to wrestle with.

GreenShelf is a noticeboard, not a shop. We never touch the money or the books. You and the other family do the rest.

1

Find a book

Search by grade, subject or school. Every listing shows the condition and the area it is in.

2

Reach out

Message the family directly through their listing. No bidding, no commission, no waiting.

3

Pick it up

Agree a time and place that suits you both. The book gets a second year instead of a bin.
